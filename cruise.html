Value Score = weighted composite: Reviews 35% + Ship Age/Newness 25% + Kid Friendliness 20% + Price Competitiveness 20%.
Review score methodology (updated Aug 19, 2026): computed from each ship's actual Cruise Critic guest review-count breakdown (Excellent/Very Good/Average/Poor/Terrible), weighted 5-4-3-2-1 and doubled to a /10 scale — not Cruise Critic's "Editor Rating" badge, which independent spot-checks showed runs meaningfully higher than actual guest sentiment on several ships (most dramatically MSC Seascape and Norwegian Viva, both of which have unusually polarized review distributions). Mariner of the Seas uses the family's own May 2026 Cruise Critic-equivalent rating (4.3/5) instead of the fleet aggregate. Small-sample ships (e.g. Star of the Seas, still building review volume) are lower-confidence.
Space Ratio = Gross Tonnage ÷ Passengers (double occupancy). Industry standard crowding metric — higher is less crowded. ≥44 = spacious · 42–43 = comfortable · below 42 = denser.
Nightly rate is a per-person ballpark estimate (interior cabin, 2-person occupancy, pulled from live 2026-27 sailing listings). The smaller line beneath it is an all-in estimate for the family's typical pattern — base fare for 2 adults at the ship's shortest advertised sailing length, plus standard $18.50/person/day gratuities for all 3 travelers — it still excludes packages (this family typically adds ~$1,650 on priority access + beverage + dining for a 5-night sailing), flights, and excursions.
✦ drive = Galveston or New Orleans homeport (drivable from Austin — ~3.5 hrs and ~5.5 hrs respectively). Celebrity has no Texas/Louisiana homeport. MSC Seascape, Norwegian Viva, and Disney Magic all currently homeport in Galveston.
Sailed marks a ship the family has direct experience with (Mariner of the Seas, May 2026; Celebrity Edge, Jul 2025) — see the notes panel for the real trip writeup.
Cross-line Value Score note: Value Score is calibrated primarily against RC/Celebrity data; use it directionally across MSC, NCL, and Disney — pricing models and bundling (NCL Free at Sea, Disney all-inclusive feel) differ materially from RC base-fare structure.
